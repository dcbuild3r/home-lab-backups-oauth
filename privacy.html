Home Lab Backups

Privacy policy

Updated November 9, 2026.

Home Lab Backups is operated privately for its operator’s infrastructure backups. This policy describes the application’s use of Google account and Google Drive information.

Information accessed and its purpose

The application reads the authorized Google account’s email address to verify the intended account. It accesses file names, identifiers, parent folders, sizes, contents, and other file metadata needed to transfer, locate, and verify backups in the configured Google Drive destination.

These operations support account authentication, backup transfer, checkpoint management, integrity verification, and recovery.

Storage and protection

Backup captures are compressed and encrypted on an operator-controlled source host before transfer to Google Drive. The encryption password is retained separately in the operator’s password manager. Integrity and recovery operations may decrypt archives on operator-controlled systems.

OAuth credentials are kept in restricted-access credential storage on operator-controlled systems. Application client credentials are also retained in the operator’s password manager for recovery.

Services used

The backup workflow exchanges account information with Google OAuth services and backup files and metadata with Google Drive. Google processes information under its own terms and privacy policy. The website’s hosting provider may process ordinary web-request metadata under its own policies.

Home Lab Backups uses Google API information solely for the backup and recovery purposes described here. Its use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and control

Backup generations remain in the configured Google Drive destination according to the operator’s retention choices. The operator can revoke application access through Google Account connections and manage stored backup objects through Google Drive.

Contact

Contact the operator using the support email displayed on the application’s Google OAuth consent screen.
